# Insurance Policies — Sunrise Health Clinic

Sunrise Health Clinic is proud to be an in-network provider for a wide range of health insurance plans. Below you will find detailed information about the five most common insurance plans accepted at our clinic. If your plan is not listed, please call our billing department at (555) 234-5678 to verify coverage before your visit.

---

## 1. BlueCross BlueShield PPO (Preferred Provider Organization)

### Overview
BlueCross BlueShield PPO is our most commonly accepted insurance plan. As a PPO, patients have the flexibility to see any in-network provider without a referral. Sunrise Health Clinic is a preferred in-network provider for all BCBS PPO tiers.

### Copay Schedule
- **Primary Care / General Visit:** $25 copay per visit
- **Specialist Consultation:** $40 copay per visit
- **Urgent Care Visit:** $50 copay per visit
- **Preventive Care / Annual Physical:** $0 copay (fully covered)
- **Telehealth Visit:** $20 copay per session
- **Lab Work (in-office):** $15 copay for routine panels
- **Imaging (X-ray, Ultrasound):** $30 copay per study

### Deductible and Out-of-Pocket
- **Annual Deductible (Individual):** $500
- **Annual Deductible (Family):** $1,000
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum (Individual):** $4,000 per year
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum (Family):** $8,000 per year
- **Coinsurance after deductible:** 80/20 (plan pays 80%, patient pays 20%)

### Coverage Details
- **Covered Services:** Office visits, preventive care, diagnostic testing, lab work, imaging, immunizations, chronic disease management, prenatal care, well-child visits, mental health screenings
- **Not Covered:** Cosmetic procedures, experimental treatments, weight loss surgery (unless medically necessary with prior authorization), over-the-counter medications, dental and vision services (separate plans required)
- **Pre-Authorization Required For:** MRI/CT scans, specialist referrals for out-of-network providers, surgical procedures, inpatient hospital stays, advanced imaging studies
- **Referrals:** Not required for in-network specialists. Required for out-of-network specialist visits.

### Pharmacy Benefits
- **Tier 1 (Generic):** $10 copay for 30-day supply
- **Tier 2 (Preferred Brand):** $30 copay for 30-day supply
- **Tier 3 (Non-Preferred Brand):** $50 copay for 30-day supply
- **Tier 4 (Specialty):** 25% coinsurance, up to $150 per fill
- **Mail Order (90-day):** 2.5x the copay of a 30-day retail fill
- **Prior Authorization:** Required for Tier 3 and Tier 4 medications

## 2. Aetna HMO (Health Maintenance Organization)

### Overview
Aetna HMO requires patients to select a Primary Care Physician (PCP) from the in-network directory. All specialist visits require a referral from your PCP. Sunrise Health Clinic participates as both a PCP and specialist provider in the Aetna HMO network.

### Copay Schedule
- **Primary Care / General Visit:** $20 copay per visit
- **Specialist Consultation:** $35 copay per visit (referral required)
- **Urgent Care Visit:** $45 copay per visit
- **Preventive Care / Annual Physical:** $0 copay (fully covered)
- **Telehealth Visit:** $15 copay per session
- **Lab Work (in-office):** $10 copay for routine panels
- **Imaging (X-ray, Ultrasound):** $25 copay per study

### Deductible and Out-of-Pocket
- **Annual Deductible (Individual):** $750
- **Annual Deductible (Family):** $1,500
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum (Individual):** $5,000 per year
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum (Family):** $10,000 per year
- **Coinsurance after deductible:** 80/20 (plan pays 80%, patient pays 20%)

## 3. UnitedHealthcare Choice Plus (POS — Point of Service)

### Overview
UnitedHealthcare Choice Plus is a flexible Point of Service (POS) plan that combines features of both HMO and PPO plans. Patients can see in-network providers without a referral, but will pay less with a PCP referral. Out-of-network coverage is available at higher cost. Sunrise Health Clinic is a Tier 1 preferred provider.

- **Specialist Consultation (with referral):** $40 copay per visit
- **Specialist Consultation (without referral):** $50 copay per visit
- **Urgent Care Visit:** $50 copay per visit
- **Preventive Care / Annual Physical:** $0 copay (fully covered)
- **Telehealth Visit:** $25 copay per session
- **Lab Work (in-office):** $20 copay for routine panels
- **Imaging (X-ray, Ultrasound):** $35 copay per study

### Deductible and Out-of-Pocket

- **Annual Deductible — In-Network (Family):** $2,000
- **Annual Deductible — Out-of-Network (Individual):** $2,000
- **Annual Deductible — Out-of-Network (Family):** $4,000
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum — In-Network (Individual):** $5,500 per year
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum — In-Network (Family):** $11,000 per year

- **Mail Order (90-day):** 2.5x the copay of a 30-day retail fill
- **Step Therapy:** Required for certain drug classes. Patient must try lower-cost alternatives before plan covers higher-tier medications.

## 5. Medicare Advantage (Medicare Part C — Sunrise Select Plan)

### Overview
Sunrise Health Clinic proudly accepts Medicare Advantage plans. Our Sunrise Select Medicare Advantage Plan is offered through our partnership with a national Medicare Advantage organization. It combines Original Medicare (Parts A and B) with additional benefits. All of our providers are enrolled as Medicare-participating physicians.

### Copay Schedule
- **Primary Care / General Visit:** $20 copay per visit
- **Specialist Consultation:** $40 copay per visit
- **Urgent Care Visit:** $45 copay per visit
- **Preventive Care / Annual Wellness Visit:** $0 copay (fully covered)
- **Telehealth Visit:** $0 copay (fully covered for Medicare beneficiaries)
- **Lab Work (in-office):** $0 copay for Medicare-covered lab services
- **Imaging (X-ray):** $20 copay per study
- **Imaging (Ultrasound, Advanced):** $50 copay per study

### Deductible and Out-of-Pocket
- **Annual Deductible (Medical):** $250
- **Annual Deductible (Prescription Drug — Part D):** $150
- **Out-of-Pocket Maximum (Individual):** $3,400 per year (in-network)
- **Coinsurance after deductible:** 80/20 (plan pays 80%, patient pays 20%)

### Coverage Details
- **Covered Services:** All Original Medicare benefits plus: routine vision exam (1 per year), routine hearing exam (1 per year), dental preventive services (2 cleanings per year), fitness benefit (SilverSneakers membership), over-the-counter health products allowance ($50/quarter), telehealth unlimited, transportation to medical appointments (24 one-way trips per year)
- **Not Covered:** Cosmetic procedures, long-term custodial care, most dental restorative procedures (separate dental rider available), private-duty nursing
- **Pre-Authorization Required For:** Inpatient hospital stays, skilled nursing facility admissions, home health services, durable medical equipment, power mobility devices, non-emergency ambulance transport, certain outpatient procedures
- **Referrals:** Not required for most in-network specialists. PCP coordination encouraged but not mandatory.

### Pharmacy Benefits (Part D Integrated)
- **Tier 1 (Preferred Generic):** $5 copay for 30-day supply
- **Tier 2 (Generic):** $12 copay for 30-day supply
- **Tier 3 (Preferred Brand):** $35 copay for 30-day supply
- **Tier 4 (Non-Preferred Brand):** $80 copay for 30-day supply
- **Tier 5 (Specialty):** 25% coinsurance
- **Coverage Gap (Donut Hole):** Once total drug costs reach $5,030, patient pays 25% of brand-name and generic drug costs until catastrophic coverage begins.
- **Catastrophic Coverage:** After $8,000 in true out-of-pocket costs, patient pays $0 for covered drugs for the rest of the year.
- **Low-Income Subsidy (Extra Help):** Patients who qualify may pay as little as $0 for prescriptions.

## General Insurance Information

### Insurance Verification
Sunrise Health Clinic verifies insurance eligibility for all scheduled appointments at least 48 hours in advance. Patients are encouraged to:
- Bring their insurance card to every visit
- Notify us of any insurance changes before their appointment
- Provide their insurance member ID and group number when scheduling

### Self-Pay and Uninsured Patients
For patients without insurance coverage, Sunrise Health Clinic offers a self-pay discount program:
- **General Visit:** $150 (discounted from $200)
- **Specialist Visit:** $225 (discounted from $300)
- **Annual Physical:** $200 (discounted from $275)
- **Lab Work:** Pricing varies; ask for a quote before services
- **Payment plans** available for balances over $200 (interest-free, up to 12 months)

### Billing and Claims
- All insurance claims are submitted electronically within 24 hours of the visit
- Patients receive an Explanation of Benefits (EOB) from their insurer after claim processing
- Any patient responsibility (copay, coinsurance, deductible) is collected at the time of service when possible
- Outstanding balances are billed monthly with 30-day payment terms
- For billing questions, contact our billing department at (555) 234-5678 or billing@sunrisehealthclinic.com

### Out-of-Network Coverage
If your insurance plan is not listed above, we may still be able to see you as an out-of-network provider. Out-of-network visits typically have higher copays and deductibles. We recommend contacting your insurance company to understand your out-of-network benefits before scheduling.

### Pre-Authorization Process
For services requiring pre-authorization:
1. Your provider at Sunrise Health Clinic will submit the pre-authorization request to your insurance company
2. Typical processing time is 3-5 business days for non-urgent requests
3. Urgent pre-authorizations are processed within 24-72 hours
4. You will be notified by phone once authorization is approved or denied
5. If denied, our staff will work with you on an appeal or alternative treatment plan

### Coordination of Benefits
If you have coverage under more than one insurance plan (for example, through your employer and your spouse's employer), please inform our billing department so we can coordinate benefits and ensure maximum coverage for your visits.
