## Appointment and Cancellation Policy

### Scheduling
Appointments can be scheduled by phone at (555) 234-5678, through the patient portal, or via Maya, our AI voice receptionist. We recommend scheduling routine visits at least 1-2 weeks in advance and annual physicals 2-3 weeks in advance.

### Appointment Confirmation
You will receive an appointment confirmation via text message and email at the time of booking. A reminder will be sent 48 hours before your appointment and again 2 hours before your scheduled time.

### Cancellation Policy
We require at least **24 hours notice** for appointment cancellations. This allows us to offer the time slot to another patient who may need care.

- **Cancellations with 24+ hours notice:** No charge.
- **Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice:** Subject to a **$50 late cancellation fee.**
- **No-show (failure to appear without notice):** A **$50 no-show fee** will be applied to your account.
- **Three no-shows within 12 months** may result in a review of your patient status with possible discharge from the practice.

### Late Arrival Policy
If you arrive more than 15 minutes late for your appointment, you may be asked to reschedule, depending on the provider's schedule. If the provider can still see you, the visit may be shortened. Please call ahead if you are running late so we can advise you accordingly.

### Same-Day Appointments
A limited number of same-day appointment slots are reserved for urgent concerns. Call (555) 234-5678 and select the urgent triage option to determine if a same-day appointment is appropriate for your situation.

## Payment Policies

### Copays and Payment
- **Copays are due at the time of service.** Please be prepared to pay your copay at check-in before seeing the provider.
- We accept the following payment methods: cash, personal check, Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, debit cards, and HSA/FSA cards.
- We do not accept payment by Venmo, PayPal, Zelle, or cryptocurrency.

### Insurance Billing
- All insurance claims are submitted electronically within 24 hours of your visit.
- You are responsible for understanding your insurance benefits, including copays, deductibles, and coinsurance.
- Any patient responsibility identified after insurance processing will be billed to you with net-30 payment terms.

### Self-Pay Pricing
For patients without insurance:
- General Visit: $150
- Specialist Visit: $225
- Annual Physical: $200
- Lab Work: Pricing varies (request quote before services)
- Imaging: Pricing varies (request quote before services)

### Payment Plans
- Available for balances over $200.
- Interest-free for up to 12 months.
- Must be arranged through the billing department before the balance is sent to collections.

### Outstanding Balances
- Statements are mailed monthly for outstanding balances.
- Balances not paid within 90 days may be referred to a third-party collections agency.
- Patients with outstanding balances over $500 may be asked to pay the balance before scheduling new appointments.

### Balance Disputes
If you believe there is an error on your bill, contact our billing department at (555) 234-5678 or billing@sunrisehealthclinic.com within 30 days of receiving the statement. We will review the charge and resolve the dispute within 10 business days.

## COVID-19 Protocols

### Current Masking Policy
As of 2025, masking is **optional** for patients and staff. We recommend wearing a mask if you are experiencing respiratory symptoms (cough, runny nose, sore throat). Masks are available at the front desk upon request.

### COVID-19 Testing
- **Rapid antigen test:** Results in 15 minutes. Cost: $25 self-pay; $0 with most insurance.
- **PCR test:** Results in 24-48 hours. Cost: $50 self-pay; $0 with most insurance.
- Testing is available by appointment Monday through Friday. Call ahead to schedule.

### COVID-19 Vaccination
- COVID-19 vaccines and updated boosters are available for all eligible patients aged 6 months and older.
- Walk-in vaccination is accepted during business hours.
- Appointment scheduling is preferred to ensure vaccine availability and minimal wait time.

### Sick Visit Procedures
- If you have respiratory symptoms, please call our office before arriving.
- We offer telehealth visits for patients with suspected COVID-19 or respiratory illness.
- If an in-person sick visit is necessary, you will be directed to a dedicated exam room with enhanced infection control measures.

## Patient Rights and Responsibilities

### Your Rights as a Patient
You have the right to:
1. Receive respectful, nondiscriminatory care regardless of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, or ability to pay.
2. Be informed about your diagnosis, treatment options, and prognosis in terms you understand.
3. Participate in decisions about your care and refuse treatment.
4. Access your medical records and request amendments.
5. Have your medical information kept confidential in accordance with HIPAA.
6. File a complaint or grievance without fear of retaliation.
7. Receive an estimate of charges before services are rendered.
8. Request an interpreter or translation services at no additional cost.

### Your Responsibilities as a Patient
You are responsible for:
1. Providing accurate and complete medical history and personal information.
2. Informing your provider of all medications, supplements, and allergies.
3. Following the treatment plan agreed upon with your provider, or informing them if you cannot comply.
4. Keeping scheduled appointments or canceling within the required notice period.
5. Paying copays, deductibles, and any outstanding balances in a timely manner.
6. Treating clinic staff and other patients with respect and courtesy.
7. Notifying our office of changes in insurance, address, phone number, or emergency contacts.

## Privacy and HIPAA Notice

### Notice of Privacy Practices
Sunrise Health Clinic is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your protected health information (PHI) in compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) of 1996.

### How We Use and Disclose Your Information
We may use or disclose your PHI for:
- **Treatment:** Sharing information with other providers involved in your care.
- **Payment:** Submitting claims to your insurance company.
- **Healthcare Operations:** Quality improvement, auditing, staff training.
- **As Required by Law:** Court orders, public health reporting, law enforcement requests.

### Your Privacy Rights
- You may request restrictions on how your PHI is used or disclosed.
- You may request to receive communications by alternative means (e.g., calling your cell phone instead of home phone).
- You may access, inspect, and obtain copies of your medical records.
- You may request amendments to your medical records.
- You may request an accounting of disclosures of your PHI.

### Breach Notification
In the event of a breach of your unsecured PHI, we will notify you in writing within 60 days, as required by law.

### Contact for Privacy Concerns
If you have questions or concerns about your privacy, contact our Privacy Officer at privacy@sunrisehealthclinic.com or (555) 234-5680.

## Telehealth Policy

### Availability
Telehealth visits are available for many appointment types including:
- Follow-up visits for chronic conditions
- Medication management consultations
- Minor illness evaluations (cold, flu, allergies, rashes)
- Mental health check-ins
- Post-procedure follow-ups
- COVID-19 symptom evaluation

### Not Appropriate for Telehealth
The following require an in-person visit:
- Initial physical examinations
- Annual wellness exams
- Procedures (biopsies, immunizations, blood draws)
- Conditions requiring hands-on assessment
- Sports physicals and medical clearance exams

### Technical Requirements
- A device with a camera and microphone (smartphone, tablet, or computer).
- Stable internet connection.
- A quiet, private location for the consultation.
- You will receive a secure video link via email or text before your appointment.

### Telehealth Billing
Telehealth visits are billed at the same rate as in-person visits. Most insurance plans now cover telehealth services with the same copay as an office visit. Self-pay telehealth visits may have a reduced rate — ask when scheduling.

## Prescription Refill Policy

### Routine Refills
- Please request refills at least **48 hours (2 business days)** before you run out of medication.
- Refills can be requested through the patient portal, by phone, or by having your pharmacy send a refill request to our office.
- Routine refills are processed Monday through Friday during business hours.

### Controlled Substance Policy
- Controlled substances (Schedule II-V) are prescribed according to state and federal regulations.
- Schedule II medications require an electronic prescription sent directly to your pharmacy (no paper scripts or phone call-ins).
- Refills of controlled substances typically require an office visit every 3-6 months for ongoing monitoring.
- We do not refill controlled substance prescriptions after hours or on weekends except in documented emergencies.

### New Prescriptions
- New prescriptions require an office visit (in-person or telehealth) with a provider.
- Prescriptions cannot be written based on a phone call alone for new medications.

### Prior Authorization
- If your insurance requires prior authorization for a medication, our staff will handle the paperwork.
- Processing time is typically 3-5 business days.
- You will be notified when the authorization is approved or denied.

## Emergency Procedures

### In-Office Medical Emergencies
Our clinical staff is trained in BLS (Basic Life Support) and our providers are ACLS (Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support) certified. We maintain emergency equipment including an AED (automated external defibrillator), oxygen, and emergency medications on-site.

### When to Call 911
Call 911 immediately for:
- Chest pain or pressure
- Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
- Signs of stroke (facial drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech)
- Severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis)
- Loss of consciousness
- Uncontrolled bleeding
- Severe head trauma
- Seizures in a non-epileptic patient

### After-Hours Emergencies
For after-hours medical concerns that are not life-threatening, call (555) 234-5678. The answering service will connect you with our on-call provider within 30 minutes for guidance.

## Additional Policies

### Weapons Policy
Firearms and weapons are not permitted on clinic premises, with the exception of law enforcement officers on duty.

### Service Animals
Service animals are welcome in the clinic. Emotional support animals and pets are not permitted in clinical areas. Please notify us in advance if you will be accompanied by a service animal.

### Photography and Recording
Photography and video/audio recording are not permitted in clinical areas, waiting rooms, or exam rooms without prior written consent from all parties present.

### Smoking and Tobacco
Sunrise Health Clinic is a smoke-free and tobacco-free campus. Smoking, vaping, and use of tobacco products are prohibited on all clinic property including the parking lot.

### Minor Patients
Patients under the age of 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian for all visits. Emancipated minors may consent to their own care with proper documentation. Minors aged 12 and older may consent to certain confidential services (reproductive health, mental health, substance abuse) as allowed by state law.
