# Medical Services — Sunrise Health Clinic

Sunrise Health Clinic offers a comprehensive range of medical services for patients of all ages. Our team of board-certified physicians provides personalized care in a modern, comfortable setting. Below is a detailed guide to our available services.

---

## 1. General Health Checkup

**Description:** A thorough evaluation of your overall health status. Includes vital signs measurement (blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, weight, BMI), review of current medications, discussion of health concerns, and a focused physical examination.

**Duration:** 20-30 minutes
**Cost Range:** $100-$150 (insurance copay typically $20-$30)
**What to Expect:** Your provider will review your medical history, check vitals, perform a physical exam, and discuss any health concerns. Follow-up tests or referrals may be ordered based on findings.
**Preparation:** Bring a list of current medications and any recent lab results from other providers. No fasting required.

---

## 2. Annual Physical Examination

**Description:** A comprehensive head-to-toe wellness exam recommended once per year. Includes complete vital signs, body systems review, age-appropriate cancer screenings, cardiovascular risk assessment, mental health screening, and a personalized wellness plan.

**Duration:** 45-60 minutes
**Cost Range:** $175-$275 (most insurance plans cover at $0 copay as preventive care)
**What to Expect:** Thorough examination of all body systems, review of immunization status, discussion of lifestyle factors (diet, exercise, sleep, stress), age-appropriate screening recommendations, and lab work orders as needed.
**Preparation:** Fast for 8-12 hours if bloodwork is expected. Bring your insurance card, medication list, and any health questions you want to discuss. Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing.

---

## 3. Pediatric Wellness Visits

**Description:** Well-child check-ups following the AAP (American Academy of Pediatrics) schedule. Includes growth and development monitoring, developmental milestone assessments, immunizations per CDC schedule, vision and hearing screenings, and age-appropriate anticipatory guidance for parents.

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes
**Cost Range:** $125-$200 (covered at $0 copay under most plans as preventive care)
**What to Expect:** Height, weight, and head circumference measurements; developmental screening questionnaires; immunization administration; discussion of nutrition, sleep, safety, and behavioral topics appropriate to the child's age.
**Preparation:** Bring your child's immunization records, any growth charts from previous providers, and a list of questions or concerns. Dress your child in easy-to-remove clothing.

---

## 4. Cardiology Consultation

**Description:** Specialized evaluation of cardiovascular health by Dr. Sarah Patel (Cardiology). Includes detailed cardiac history review, cardiovascular physical examination, risk factor assessment, EKG interpretation, and development of a cardiac care plan.

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes
**Cost Range:** $200-$350 (specialist copay typically $35-$50)
**What to Expect:** In-depth discussion of cardiac symptoms (chest pain, shortness of breath, palpitations, dizziness), family cardiac history review, physical examination with cardiac focus, EKG if indicated, and discussion of treatment options or further testing.
**Preparation:** Bring a list of all medications including supplements, any previous cardiac test results, and a written list of symptoms including when they occur and how long they last. Avoid caffeine for 4 hours before your visit if an EKG is planned.

---

## 5. EKG/ECG Testing

**Description:** Electrocardiogram — a non-invasive test that records the electrical activity of the heart. Used to detect heart rhythm abnormalities (arrhythmias), heart attack evidence, enlarged heart chambers, and other cardiac conditions.

**Duration:** 10-15 minutes for the test itself; 20-30 minutes including interpretation and discussion
**Cost Range:** $50-$150 for the test (insurance copay applies; many plans cover diagnostic EKG after deductible)
**What to Expect:** Small electrode patches are placed on your chest, arms, and legs. You lie still for about 60 seconds while the machine records your heart rhythm. The test is completely painless. Results are typically available immediately and reviewed by your provider during the same visit.
**Preparation:** Wear a two-piece outfit for easy chest access. Avoid applying lotions or oils to your chest area. Avoid vigorous exercise for 30 minutes before the test.

---

## 6. Dermatology — Skin Examination

**Description:** Comprehensive skin evaluation by Dr. Michael Chen (Dermatology). Includes full-body skin cancer screening, evaluation of moles and skin lesions, diagnosis of skin conditions (eczema, psoriasis, rashes), and treatment planning.

**Duration:** 20-30 minutes
**Cost Range:** $175-$300 (specialist copay typically $35-$50)
**What to Expect:** Visual examination of skin from head to toe, dermoscopy (magnified examination) of suspicious moles or lesions, discussion of skin care concerns, and treatment recommendations. Biopsy may be recommended for concerning lesions.
**Preparation:** Remove nail polish from fingers and toes. Come with minimal makeup. Note any moles or spots that have changed in size, shape, or color.

---

## 7. Dermatology — Acne Treatment

**Description:** Targeted evaluation and treatment plan for acne of all severity levels. Includes assessment of acne type (comedonal, inflammatory, cystic), current skincare routine review, prescription treatment planning, and follow-up scheduling.

**Duration:** 20-30 minutes
**Cost Range:** $150-$250 (specialist copay applies)
**What to Expect:** Examination of affected areas, discussion of current skincare products, development of a multi-step treatment plan which may include topical medications, oral medications, or procedural interventions. Follow-up visits are typically scheduled at 6-8 week intervals to monitor progress.
**Preparation:** Come to the appointment with a clean face (no makeup if possible). Bring a list of current skincare products and any previously tried treatments.

---

## 8. Orthopedic Evaluation

**Description:** Musculoskeletal assessment by Dr. David Kim (Orthopedics). Evaluates bones, joints, muscles, ligaments, and tendons. Covers acute injuries (sprains, fractures, dislocations) and chronic conditions (arthritis, tendinitis, back pain).

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes
**Cost Range:** $200-$350 (specialist copay typically $35-$50)
**What to Expect:** Detailed history of your musculoskeletal complaint, physical examination including range of motion testing, strength testing, and special orthopedic tests. X-rays may be ordered on-site. Treatment options discussed may include physical therapy, medication, bracing, injections, or surgical referral.
**Preparation:** Wear shorts or loose clothing that allows access to the affected area. Bring any prior imaging (X-rays, MRI discs) and reports from other providers.

---

## 9. Sports Medicine Consultation

**Description:** Specialized evaluation for athletes and active individuals. Covers sports injury assessment, return-to-play clearance, performance optimization, and injury prevention strategies. Provided by Dr. David Kim.

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes
**Cost Range:** $200-$325 (specialist copay applies)
**What to Expect:** Activity-specific injury assessment, biomechanical evaluation, discussion of training regimens, development of rehabilitation or prevention program, and clearance recommendations for return to sport.
**Preparation:** Wear athletic clothing. Bring your sports/athletic shoes. Document your training schedule and when symptoms occur.

---

## 10. OB-GYN Services — Well-Woman Exam

**Description:** Annual gynecological examination by Dr. Emily Rodriguez (OB-GYN). Includes breast exam, pelvic exam, Pap smear (per screening guidelines), STI screening as appropriate, contraception counseling, and reproductive health discussion.

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes
**Cost Range:** $175-$275 (covered at $0 copay under most plans as preventive care)
**What to Expect:** Review of menstrual history and reproductive health, breast examination, pelvic examination with Pap smear if due, discussion of contraceptive options, and screening for sexually transmitted infections if indicated.
**Preparation:** Schedule the appointment for a time when you are not on your period if possible. Avoid douching or using vaginal creams for 48 hours before the exam. Bring a list of any symptoms or questions.

---

## 11. OB-GYN Services — Prenatal Care

**Description:** Comprehensive pregnancy monitoring by Dr. Emily Rodriguez. Includes initial prenatal assessment, monthly check-ups (increasing frequency in third trimester), ultrasound coordination, gestational diabetes screening, and birth planning.

**Duration:** 15-30 minutes per routine visit; 60 minutes for initial prenatal visit
**Cost Range:** $150-$250 per visit (prenatal care is covered under most insurance plans; global maternity fee may apply)
**What to Expect:** Regular monitoring of maternal and fetal health including weight, blood pressure, fetal heart rate, fundal height measurement, urine testing, and blood work at specific intervals. Ultrasound referrals coordinated at appropriate gestational ages.
**Preparation:** For the initial visit, bring a list of medications, prenatal vitamins, prior pregnancy records, and your last menstrual period date. Drink water before visits as a full bladder may be needed for early ultrasounds.

---

## 12. ENT Consultation

**Description:** Ear, nose, and throat evaluation by Dr. Robert Thompson (ENT/Otolaryngology). Covers hearing issues, sinus problems, throat complaints, allergies, sleep apnea screening, and head/neck concerns.

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes
**Cost Range:** $200-$350 (specialist copay typically $35-$50)
**What to Expect:** Targeted examination using specialized instruments (otoscope for ears, nasal endoscopy if indicated), hearing assessment, discussion of symptoms, and treatment recommendations which may include medications, allergy management, or referral for further testing (sleep study, CT sinus).
**Preparation:** Bring a list of symptoms with their duration and frequency. Note any known allergies or prior ENT treatments. If you have hearing concerns, bring a companion who can provide observations about your hearing.

---

## 13. Internal Medicine Consultation

**Description:** Comprehensive adult medicine evaluation by Dr. Aisha Hassan (Internal Medicine). Specializes in complex medical conditions, chronic disease management (diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders), and multi-system health assessments.

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes
**Cost Range:** $175-$325 (specialist copay applies for referred visits; primary care copay for established patients)
**What to Expect:** Thorough review of medical history and all active conditions, medication management review, physical examination, lab work orders as needed, and development of a coordinated care plan addressing all health issues.
**Preparation:** Bring all medication bottles (not just a list), any home monitoring logs (blood sugar, blood pressure), and recent lab results from other providers.

---

## 14. Allergy Testing

**Description:** Identification of environmental and food allergies through skin prick testing or blood testing (specific IgE panels). Helps identify triggers for allergic rhinitis, asthma, eczema, and food allergies.

**Duration:** 45-60 minutes (skin prick test requires 15-20 minute observation period)
**Cost Range:** $150-$400 depending on the number of allergens tested (insurance covers after deductible; some plans require prior authorization)
**What to Expect:** For skin prick testing, small amounts of common allergens are applied to the forearm or back with tiny scratches. Results are read after 15-20 minutes. Blood testing involves a standard blood draw with results available in 5-7 business days.
**Preparation:** Stop taking antihistamines (Claritin, Zyrtec, Benadryl) for 5-7 days before skin testing. Do not stop asthma medications. Blood testing does not require medication changes.

---

## 15. Immunizations and Vaccinations

**Description:** Administration of recommended vaccines for all ages. Includes childhood immunizations (per CDC schedule), adult boosters (Tdap, flu), travel vaccines, shingles vaccine, pneumococcal vaccine, HPV vaccine, and COVID-19 vaccines and boosters.

**Duration:** 10-15 minutes per vaccination (plus 15-minute observation period)
**Cost Range:** $0-$75 per vaccine (most preventive vaccines covered at $0 under ACA-compliant plans; some travel vaccines are out-of-pocket)
**What to Expect:** Brief health screening, vaccine administration by injection, 15-minute post-vaccination monitoring for allergic reactions, and updated immunization record.
**Preparation:** Bring your immunization records. Inform staff of any prior vaccine reactions or egg allergies. Wear a short-sleeved shirt for easy arm access.

---

## 16. Laboratory Services (Blood Work)

**Description:** On-site blood draw and specimen collection for diagnostic testing. Common panels include Complete Blood Count (CBC), Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP), Lipid Panel, Thyroid Function (TSH, T3, T4), Hemoglobin A1C, Vitamin D level, and Urinalysis.

**Duration:** 10-15 minutes for the blood draw; results available in 1-3 business days
**Cost Range:** $25-$200 depending on panels ordered (insurance copay typically $10-$20 for routine labs; some preventive labs covered at $0)
**What to Expect:** Standard venipuncture blood draw from the arm. Results are sent to your provider and made available through the patient portal. Abnormal results trigger a follow-up call from your provider's office.
**Preparation:** Fast for 8-12 hours for lipid panel and glucose testing. Drink plenty of water to make the blood draw easier. Continue taking medications unless your provider specifically instructs otherwise.

---

## 17. Diagnostic Imaging (X-ray and Ultrasound)

**Description:** On-site X-ray and ultrasound imaging for diagnostic purposes. X-rays are available for bone fractures, chest imaging, and joint evaluations. Ultrasound is available for abdominal, thyroid, musculoskeletal, and OB-GYN imaging. MRI and CT scans are referred to our affiliated imaging center.

**Duration:** 15-30 minutes for the imaging study; preliminary results same day, final radiology report within 1-2 business days
**Cost Range:** X-ray: $75-$200; Ultrasound: $150-$400 (insurance copay typically $20-$50)
**What to Expect:** For X-ray, you may be asked to change into a gown and remove jewelry. For ultrasound, gel is applied to the skin and a probe is moved over the area of interest. Both are painless and non-invasive.
**Preparation:** For abdominal ultrasound, fast for 6-8 hours. For pelvic ultrasound, drink 32 ounces of water one hour before the appointment and do not empty your bladder. X-rays generally require no preparation.

---

## 18. Minor Procedures (Wound Care and Biopsies)

**Description:** In-office minor surgical procedures including wound cleaning and closure (sutures/staples), abscess incision and drainage, skin biopsy (shave, punch, or excisional), mole removal, ingrown toenail treatment, foreign body removal, and cyst excision.

**Duration:** 15-45 minutes depending on the procedure
**Cost Range:** $150-$500 depending on complexity (insurance covers after copay and deductible; some procedures may require prior authorization)
**What to Expect:** Local anesthesia is administered to numb the area. The procedure is performed in our clean procedure room. Post-procedure wound care instructions are provided, and follow-up is scheduled as needed for suture removal or pathology results review.
**Preparation:** Eat a light meal before the procedure to prevent lightheadedness. Inform your provider of any blood-thinning medications (aspirin, warfarin, Eliquis) as these may need to be held before the procedure. Arrange for someone to drive you home if the procedure is on a hand or foot.
