# Patient Frequently Asked Questions — Sunrise Health Clinic

Below are answers to the most common questions our patients ask. If your question is not addressed here, please call us at (555) 234-5678 or speak with Maya, our AI receptionist.

---

## Scheduling and Appointments

### Q: How do I book an appointment?
**A:** You can book an appointment in several ways: (1) Call our office at (555) 234-5678 during business hours (Monday-Friday 8 AM to 6 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 1 PM). (2) Speak with Maya, our AI voice receptionist, available on our website. (3) Log into the patient portal at portal.sunrisehealthclinic.com and schedule online. (4) Walk-in appointments are available on a limited basis for urgent concerns.

### Q: How do I cancel an appointment?
**A:** Please cancel at least 24 hours before your scheduled appointment to avoid a $50 no-show fee. You can cancel by calling (555) 234-5678, through the patient portal, or by speaking with Maya. Same-day cancellations may be subject to the no-show fee unless there is a medical emergency.

### Q: How do I reschedule an appointment?
**A:** Call our office at (555) 234-5678 or use the patient portal to reschedule. We ask for at least 24 hours notice when rescheduling. Our staff will find the next available slot that works for your schedule.

### Q: Do you accept walk-in patients?
**A:** Yes, we accept walk-in patients on a limited basis for urgent concerns. Walk-in availability depends on the day's schedule and is first-come, first-served. For non-urgent matters, we strongly recommend scheduling an appointment in advance to minimize wait times. Walk-in hours are Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 4 PM.

### Q: What is the typical wait time for an appointment?
**A:** For primary care (Dr. Wilson), most patients can be seen within 3-5 business days for routine visits, or same-day for urgent concerns. Specialist appointments (Cardiology, Dermatology, Orthopedics, OB-GYN, ENT) typically have a 1-2 week wait for new patients and 3-7 days for established patients. Annual physicals should be scheduled 2-3 weeks in advance.

### Q: Can I request a specific doctor?
**A:** Absolutely. When booking your appointment, simply let us know which doctor you would like to see. We will check their availability and schedule you accordingly. If your preferred doctor is unavailable on your desired date, we can offer alternative dates or suggest another qualified provider.

### Q: What are your hours of operation?
**A:** Sunrise Health Clinic is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays and major holidays (New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas).

### Q: Do you offer evening or weekend appointments?
**A:** We offer Saturday morning appointments from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM for patients who cannot visit during the work week. We do not currently offer evening appointments. We are working to expand our hours in the future.

## Insurance and Billing

### Q: What insurance plans do you accept?
**A:** We accept most major insurance plans including BlueCross BlueShield PPO, Aetna HMO, UnitedHealthcare Choice Plus, Cigna Open Access Plus, and Medicare Advantage. We also accept many other plans. Please call our billing department at (555) 234-5678 to verify your specific plan before your visit.

### Q: What if I do not have insurance?
**A:** We offer a self-pay discount program for uninsured patients. General visits are $150 (discounted from $200), specialist visits are $225 (discounted from $300), and annual physicals are $200 (discounted from $275). Payment plans are available for balances over $200, interest-free for up to 12 months.

### Q: When is my copay due?
**A:** Copays are collected at the time of your visit, before you see the provider. We accept cash, check, credit card, debit card, and HSA/FSA cards. Please bring your insurance card to every visit so we can verify your copay amount.

### Q: Do you offer payment plans?
**A:** Yes. For balances over $200, we offer interest-free payment plans for up to 12 months. Please speak with our billing department to set up a payment plan. We work with patients to find affordable payment arrangements.

### Q: How do I get an itemized bill or receipt?
**A:** You can request an itemized statement by calling our billing department at (555) 234-5678 or through the patient portal. Itemized bills are also mailed upon request. Most insurance companies also provide an Explanation of Benefits (EOB) detailing charges and payments.

### Q: Do I need a referral to see a specialist?
**A:** It depends on your insurance plan. HMO plans (like Aetna HMO) generally require a referral from your primary care physician. PPO plans (like BlueCross BlueShield PPO) and POS plans typically do not require referrals for in-network specialists. Check with your insurance company or ask our staff when scheduling.

### Q: Do you accept Medicaid?
**A:** We do not currently accept Medicaid. However, we accept Medicare Advantage plans. Uninsured or underinsured patients may qualify for our self-pay discount program. Please contact our billing department to discuss options.

### Q: What happens if my insurance claim is denied?
**A:** If your claim is denied, our billing department will review the denial reason and contact your insurance company on your behalf. Many denials can be resolved by providing additional documentation. If the denial is upheld, we will discuss your options, including filing an appeal or setting up a self-pay arrangement.

### Q: Do you offer telehealth appointments?
**A:** Yes, we offer telehealth video visits for many appointment types including follow-up visits, medication management, minor illness consultations, and mental health check-ins. Telehealth appointments are available Monday through Friday during regular business hours. To schedule a telehealth visit, let us know when booking and we will send you a secure video link.

### Q: What should I do if I have a medical emergency?
**A:** If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 immediately or go to the nearest emergency room. Our clinic is not an emergency facility. For urgent but non-emergency concerns during business hours, call our office at (555) 234-5678 and select the urgent triage option. For after-hours urgent concerns, our answering service can connect you with the on-call provider.

### Q: What is your after-hours policy?
**A:** After regular business hours, our phone line connects to an answering service. For urgent medical questions, the answering service will page the on-call provider who will return your call within 30 minutes. For non-urgent matters, please leave a message and our staff will return your call the next business day.

## New Patients

### Q: What should I bring to my first appointment?
**A:** New patients should bring: (1) A valid photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport). (2) Your insurance card(s). (3) A complete list of current medications, including dosages and supplements. (4) Any relevant medical records or test results from previous providers. (5) A list of allergies (medications, food, environmental). (6) Your completed new patient paperwork (downloadable from our website or available upon arrival).

### Q: How early should I arrive for my first visit?
**A:** Please arrive 15-20 minutes before your scheduled appointment time to complete new patient paperwork and insurance verification. If you have downloaded and completed the forms from our website in advance, arriving 10 minutes early is sufficient.

### Q: How do I transfer my medical records to Sunrise Health Clinic?
**A:** You can request a medical records transfer from your previous provider by: (1) Signing a records release form (available at our front desk or on our website). (2) Your previous provider will fax or electronically transfer records to us. (3) Typical transfer time is 5-10 business days. (4) You can also bring physical copies of your records to your first appointment.

### Q: Can I see a specialist as a new patient without seeing a primary care doctor first?
**A:** In most cases, yes. If your insurance plan does not require a referral (PPO, POS, or self-pay), you can book directly with a specialist. If your plan requires a referral (HMO), you will need to establish care with a primary care physician first and obtain a referral.

## Medical Records and Patient Portal

### Q: How do I access the patient portal?
**A:** Visit portal.sunrisehealthclinic.com and click "Register" if you are a new user. You will need your date of birth and the email address on file at our clinic. The portal allows you to view test results, message your provider, request prescription refills, schedule appointments, and access your medical records.

### Q: How do I get copies of my medical records?
**A:** You can request your medical records by: (1) Logging into the patient portal and downloading records directly. (2) Filling out a medical records release form at the front desk. (3) Calling (555) 234-5678 and requesting records by phone. Records are typically available within 5-7 business days. There is no charge for electronic records. Paper copies may incur a small copying fee.

### Q: Are my medical records secure?
**A:** Yes. Sunrise Health Clinic fully complies with HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act) regulations. Your medical records are stored in encrypted electronic health record systems. Access is limited to authorized clinical staff directly involved in your care. We never share your records with third parties without your written consent, except as required by law.

## COVID-19 Protocols

### Q: What are your current COVID-19 policies?
**A:** As of 2025, our COVID-19 protocols are as follows: Masking is optional for patients and staff but recommended for patients with respiratory symptoms. Hand sanitizer stations are available throughout the clinic. We offer COVID-19 rapid testing and PCR testing by appointment. COVID-19 vaccines and boosters are available for all eligible patients.

### Q: Do you offer COVID-19 testing?
**A:** Yes. We offer both rapid antigen tests (results in 15 minutes) and PCR tests (results in 24-48 hours). Testing is available Monday through Friday by appointment. Rapid tests cost $25 for self-pay patients; most insurance plans cover testing at no cost. Please call ahead to schedule a testing appointment so we can prepare a safe testing area.

### Q: Can I get a COVID-19 vaccine at the clinic?
**A:** Yes. We offer COVID-19 vaccines and boosters for all eligible patients aged 6 months and older. Both mRNA (Pfizer, Moderna) and updated formulations are available based on current CDC recommendations. Walk-in vaccination is available during regular business hours, but appointments are preferred. Most insurance plans cover COVID-19 vaccines at $0 copay.

### Q: What if I have COVID symptoms and need to see a doctor?
**A:** If you have COVID-19 symptoms (fever, cough, shortness of breath, loss of taste/smell), please call our office before coming in. We offer telehealth visits for COVID-related concerns or can schedule a dedicated sick visit with enhanced safety precautions. Please do not walk into the clinic with active respiratory symptoms without calling ahead.

## Prescriptions and Medications

### Q: How do I request a prescription refill?
**A:** You can request a refill by: (1) Calling our office at (555) 234-5678 and selecting the prescription refill option. (2) Logging into the patient portal and submitting a refill request. (3) Asking your pharmacy to send a refill request to our office. Please allow 48 hours for refill processing. Controlled substance refills require an office visit.

### Q: Can prescriptions be called in to my pharmacy?
**A:** Yes. Most prescriptions are sent electronically to your preferred pharmacy. If you have a new pharmacy preference, please let us know and we will update your records. Controlled substances (Schedule II) must be sent electronically by law and cannot be called in by phone.

### Q: What if I need a prescription after hours?
**A:** For urgent medication needs after hours, our on-call provider can authorize limited prescriptions through the answering service. Non-urgent prescription requests should be submitted during business hours and will be processed the next business day. Plan ahead and request refills at least 48 hours before you run out.

### Q: Do you provide prior authorization for medications?
**A:** Yes. If your insurance requires prior authorization for a medication, our staff will submit the necessary paperwork on your behalf. The process typically takes 3-5 business days. We will notify you once the authorization is approved or if an alternative medication needs to be considered.

## Referrals and Specialist Care

### Q: How do referrals to specialists work?
**A:** When your provider determines you need specialist care, they will: (1) Discuss the referral with you and explain the reason. (2) Submit an electronic referral to the specialist (either within our clinic or to an external provider). (3) Our referral coordinator will contact you to schedule the specialist appointment. (4) All relevant medical records and test results are sent to the specialist before your appointment.

### Q: How long does a referral take?
**A:** Internal referrals (to specialists within Sunrise Health Clinic) are typically processed same-day, with appointments available within 1-2 weeks. External referrals to outside specialists take 2-3 business days to process, and scheduling depends on the external provider's availability.

### Q: Can I see a specialist outside of Sunrise Health Clinic?
**A:** Yes. If you prefer or require a specialist not available at our clinic, your provider can refer you to an external specialist. We partner with several specialty practices and hospital systems in the area. Your insurance network requirements will apply.

## Lab Work and Test Results

### Q: Do I need to fast before blood work?
**A:** Fasting is required for certain tests including lipid panels (cholesterol), fasting blood glucose, and some metabolic panels. Your provider will inform you if fasting is needed. Generally, fasting means no food or drinks (except water) for 8-12 hours before the blood draw. Continue taking your regular medications with water unless otherwise instructed.

### Q: When will I get my test results?
**A:** Routine blood work results are typically available within 1-3 business days. Results are posted to the patient portal as soon as they are reviewed by your provider. For abnormal results, your provider's office will contact you by phone. Pathology results (biopsies) take 5-7 business days. Imaging results are usually available within 1-2 business days.

### Q: How do I access my test results?
**A:** Test results are available through the patient portal at portal.sunrisehealthclinic.com. You will receive an email notification when new results are posted. You can also call our office to request results by phone. For complex results that require discussion, your provider may schedule a follow-up appointment.

### Q: What if my results are abnormal?
**A:** If any test results are outside the normal range, your provider will review them and determine the appropriate next steps. For mildly abnormal results, you may receive a message through the portal with instructions. For significantly abnormal results, our clinical staff will call you directly to discuss the findings and schedule a follow-up if needed. Critical lab values are communicated immediately by phone.

## Emergency vs. Urgent Care

### Q: When should I go to the emergency room versus the clinic?
**A:** Go to the emergency room or call 911 for: chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke (facial drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech), severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, severe allergic reactions (anaphylaxis), major trauma or broken bones, or poisoning. For non-life-threatening but urgent issues (mild to moderate illness, minor injuries, infections, rashes), our clinic can see you as an urgent walk-in or same-day appointment during business hours.

### Q: Do you have urgent care hours?
**A:** We do not operate a separate urgent care facility, but we reserve appointment slots each day for same-day urgent visits. Call (555) 234-5678 and select the urgent care option to be triaged by a nurse who will determine if a same-day appointment is appropriate. Urgent walk-ins are also accepted during business hours on a first-come, first-served basis.

### Q: What should I do for a medical concern after hours?
**A:** For life-threatening emergencies, always call 911. For urgent but non-emergency concerns after hours, call our main number at (555) 234-5678 — the answering service will connect you with our on-call provider within 30 minutes. For non-urgent concerns, leave a message and we will follow up the next business day.

## Miscellaneous

### Q: Do you provide medical clearance letters for work, school, or sports?
**A:** Yes. Our providers can issue medical clearance letters, work/school excuse notes, and sports physical forms. These may require an office visit for evaluation. Standard forms are typically completed during the visit. Complex forms (disability, FMLA) may take 5-7 business days and incur a form completion fee of $25.

### Q: Can I bring a family member or friend to my appointment?
**A:** Yes, you are welcome to bring a support person to your appointment. For pediatric visits, a parent or legal guardian must be present. For telehealth visits, support persons can join the video call. Please limit the number of companions to one person to maintain exam room comfort.

### Q: Do you have interpreting services?
**A:** Yes. We offer professional medical interpreter services at no additional cost for patients who need language assistance. Please inform us of your language needs when scheduling so we can arrange an interpreter. We can provide in-person interpreters for common languages and phone/video interpreters for all other languages.

### Q: How do I provide feedback or file a complaint?
**A:** We value your feedback. You can: (1) Fill out a patient satisfaction survey (emailed after each visit). (2) Call our patient relations coordinator at (555) 234-5679. (3) Email feedback@sunrisehealthclinic.com. (4) Submit feedback through the patient portal. All complaints are reviewed within 5 business days and you will receive a response.

### Q: Do you offer flu shots?
**A:** Yes. Flu shots are available during flu season (typically September through March) for all patients aged 6 months and older. Walk-in flu shots are welcome during business hours while supplies last. Most insurance plans cover flu shots at $0 copay. Self-pay flu shots are $35.
